1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Shadhin Transmission ("the App", "we", "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our field operations management platform. The App serves four distinct roles — Admin, Supervisor, Surveyor, and Technician — along with an HQ Inventory module, enabling coordinated task management, workflow oversight, and real-time field reporting.

By accessing or using Shadhin Transmission, you agree to the practices described in this policy. Please read it carefully.

2. User Roles & Data Access Scope

The App operates with a role-based access model. Each role has different data visibility and responsibilities:

Admin
  • Full platform oversight
  • Manage all employee accounts & roles
  • View, assign, and track all tasks
  • Access HQ Inventory reports
  • View performance dashboards
Supervisor
  • Oversee assigned team tasks
  • Monitor Surveyor & Technician activity
  • Approve or escalate field updates
  • View team-level inventory usage
Surveyor
  • Submit field survey reports
  • Upload site photos & findings
  • View own assigned tasks only
  • Log location for site visits
Technician
  • Receive and update task status
  • Log work completion & notes
  • Request inventory items
  • View own work history
Role Isolation: Users only see data relevant to their role. Surveyors and Technicians cannot access Admin dashboards or other users' records. Data access is enforced server-side.
3. Information We Collect

To deliver the App's functionality, we collect the following categories of information:

Data Type Details & Purpose Roles Affected
Account Information Name, email, phone number, employee ID, assigned role — used to create and manage accounts within the platform. All Roles
Task & Activity Logs Task assignments, status updates, completion timestamps, notes, and escalations — core to operational tracking. All Roles
Location Data GPS coordinates captured during active field operations (site visits, surveys). Used for task verification and reporting. Not tracked in background. Surveyor, Technician
Media Uploads Photos or documents uploaded as part of field reports or task submissions. Surveyor, Technician
Inventory Records Item requests, usage logs, stock movements in the HQ Inventory module. Admin, Supervisor, Technician
Device Information Device OS, app version, and crash/error logs — used for technical support and app stability only. All Roles
4. How We Use Your Information
  • To authenticate users and enforce role-based access permissions
  • To enable Admins to assign, track, and manage employee tasks across all roles
  • To allow Supervisors to monitor team progress and approve updates
  • To facilitate field data collection (surveys, site reports, task completions) by Surveyors and Technicians
  • To manage HQ Inventory — tracking item requests, allocations, and usage
  • To generate operational reports and performance overviews for Admin and Supervisor roles
  • To send in-app notifications for task assignments, updates, and alerts
  • To maintain audit trails for accountability and compliance
  • To diagnose technical issues, crashes, and improve app performance
We do not use your data for advertising, marketing, or selling to third parties. All data use is strictly limited to operating and improving the Shadhin Transmission platform.
5. Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored on secure, encrypted servers. We apply the following safeguards:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms — never stored in plaintext
  • Role-based access controls are enforced both on client and server layers
  • Audit logs track data access and changes for accountability
  • Regular security reviews and updates are conducted
  • Media uploads are stored with restricted access permissions

While we implement strong security measures, no system is completely immune to risk. Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential.

6. Location Data

Location access is used only when a Surveyor or Technician is actively performing a field task within the app. Specifically:

  • Location is captured only in the foreground — never tracked silently in the background
  • Location data is attached to specific task or survey submissions for verification purposes
  • Admins and Supervisors may view location data tied to task records — not live tracking
  • Users are notified when location is being accessed via their device's standard permission prompts
You may revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings. This may affect certain field reporting features.
7. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Limited data sharing occurs only in these circumstances:

  • Within the organization: Data is shared between roles as required for operations (e.g., Admin viewing Technician task updates)
  • Cloud infrastructure providers: Hosting and database services that process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements
  • Push notification services: To deliver in-app alerts and task notifications
  • Legal compliance: We may disclose data if required by applicable law, court order, or government authority

All third-party service providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and use it only for the specified purpose.

8. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as necessary to operate the platform and fulfil legal obligations:

  • Active employee accounts and task records are retained for the duration of employment/engagement
  • Archived task data and inventory logs are retained for up to 3 years for audit purposes
  • Upon account deactivation, personal identifiers are anonymized or deleted within 90 days, unless legal retention is required
  • Media uploads associated with closed tasks are reviewed for deletion on a rolling basis
9. Your Rights

All users of the platform have the following rights regarding their data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated personal data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Portability: Request your personal data in a portable format
  • Objection: Raise concerns about how your data is processed

To exercise any of these rights, contact the designated Admin of your organization or reach us directly via the contact details below.
